Tomas Geffner

I am a senior research scientist at NVIDIA, working on generative modeling within the Fundamental Generative AI Research Group.

I work on probabilistic machine learning, focusing on generative models and sampling methods. I am interested in methodological developments as well as applications in different scientific domains.

A little background

I obtained my PhD in Computer Science from UMass Amherst in 2023, supervised by Justin Domke.

During my PhD, I was grateful to intern at VantAI (New York, summer 2023) with Luca Naef and Michael Bronstein; Google DeepMind (London, summer 2022) with Andriy Mnih and George Papamakarios; Microsoft Research (Cambridge UK, summer 2021) with Cheng Zhang, Emre Kiciman and Miltos Allamanis; and Amazon AWS (New York, summer 2018) with Bing Xiang and Ramesh Nallapati.

Before my PhD, I also did some work on planning. Outside research, I try to play tennis.
